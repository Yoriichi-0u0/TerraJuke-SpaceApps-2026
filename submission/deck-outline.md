# Demo deck outline (7 slides maximum, including title)

1. **Title.** TerraJuke — One Earth. Every Sense. Everyone. Team BoboMimi · Bintulu, Malaysia · The Earth Information Jukebox. Live link and repo link.
2. **The problem.** NASA's Earth Information Center tells the story of Earth in pictures. "Change in Night Lights 2012–2023" is stunning, and silent for anyone who cannot see it. Screenshot of the NASA image with credit.
3. **What TerraJuke does.** Point, tap or arrow to a place: purple (more light) rises, orange (less light) falls, white holds one tone. Screenshot: India region pulsing with its caption.
4. **Every sense, one moment.** One event drives sound, a ring pulse, a caption, optional screen-reader text and optional Android vibration. Keyboard-only, reduced motion, "Listen to the legend", full Describe text. Icons for ear, eye, text, touch.
5. **Honest science.** Reads NASA's own legend from the image; masks the legend, insets, labels and wedges; insets are duplicates, so Tour plays their map boxes. Rendered colours, not radiance; pixel share, not area. Mask-overlay figure.
6. **Built and tested in the hackathon.** Repo created 14 Nov 09:10 MYT. NASA file byte-verified (SHA-256). 24 unit tests, 15 browser tests × Chromium and iPhone WebKit, zero axe violations, run on the live site.
7. **Credits and SI.** NASA SVS 5313 credits (Kel Elkins; Zhuosen Wang, Miguel Román, Ranjay Shrestha, Srija Chakraborty, Virginia Kalb; Black Marble, Suomi NPP VIIRS, NASA/NOAA). SI: code written with Claude Code under the team's direction; no SI images, audio or data. Links.
