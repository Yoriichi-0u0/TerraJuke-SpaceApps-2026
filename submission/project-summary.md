# TerraJuke — project summary (draft for the NASA project page)

**One Earth. Every Sense. Everyone.**

At night, the Earth tells a story in light. NASA's Earth Information Center shows that story on a wall-sized map: *Change in Night Lights between 2012 and 2023*, where purple marks places that grew brighter and orange marks places that grew darker. It is a beautiful picture, and for anyone who cannot see it, it is silent.

TerraJuke turns that picture into an experience for every sense. Choose a spot on the map, with a mouse, a finger or the arrow keys, and TerraJuke plays it. Places NASA coloured purple answer with a rising pair of notes; places coloured orange answer with a falling pair; steady places hum a single tone. The more of the spot that is lit, the louder and busier it sounds, and the sound moves from left to right as you travel from west to east. At the same instant a ring pulses on the map, a caption says what you just heard and why, a screen reader can announce it, and on supported Android phones you can feel it as a vibration. Nothing is carried by sound alone.

A guided Tour visits the four regions NASA highlighted (Western Europe, the Eastern Mediterranean, Ukraine and India), playing the real map pixels behind each magnified inset, and then walks the whole world. "Listen to the legend" teaches the mapping by ear, from orange through white to purple. "Describe" gives a full text description and plain-language summaries for every highlighted region.

Every sound is generated live in the browser from NASA's own pixels, using NASA's own legend read from the same image. TerraJuke is honest about what it hears: the direction of change as NASA drew it, and how far a colour sits along NASA's legend. It does not claim radiance, area, urbanization or economic activity, and it says so on the page.
